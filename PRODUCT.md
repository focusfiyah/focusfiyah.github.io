# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS (Ralph 2026-10-28), one page plus small assets, hosted free on GitHub Pages (repo focusfiyah/focusfiyah.github.io). A custom domain will point to it later. No paid services.

## Users
Anyone who wants AI help with their work and lands here from a link Ralph shares when someone asks "do you have a website?": creators and TikTok Shop sellers, small local businesses (shops, schools, trades), online brands, and people who want AI set up for them. Mostly on phones, arriving from a DM, a comment, an email signature or a business card.

## Product Purpose
Market Ralph as an AI consultant who builds and runs working AI systems. Success = a visitor understands in seconds what he builds, sees real work, and sends the contact form asking for a free consult.

## Positioning
Ralph builds it, not just advises: real systems running for real clients today (AI product videos posting on TikTok Shop, an AI phone receptionist answering a driving school's calls, an automated YouTube channel he runs himself), and he can keep running them after setup. He is a content creator himself, so he knows what gets views.

## Operating Context
Visitors judge quickly on a phone. The page shows work samples (short muted video clips, YouTube thumbnails), what he offers, and one contact form. No sales calls are pushed; the form asks what they need.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services shown: AI product videos/UGC ads at volume; AI phone receptionist / front desk (answers calls, books, sends details); automated content channels; custom tools and automations (trackers, quality checks, custom Claude skills); done-for-you setup and ongoing running.
- No prices on the page; the call to action is a free consult through the contact form.
- Contact form only (free form service), no phone number, no booking link for now.
- Clients are anonymous: "a TikTok Shop creator", "a Brooklyn driving school". No client names, handles, voices or phone numbers. Videos play muted.
- Name shown: "Ralph, AI Consultant". No photo yet (spot may be added later).
- Free only: no paid fonts, libraries, images or hosting.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Ralph, AI Consultant.
- Look requested by Ralph: bright and clean (light ground, one bold accent, work up front). Bright, neutral imagery; nothing dark or moody.
- Voice: plain words, honest, practical; humanized copy (no AI-sounding phrasing).

## Evidence on Hand
- Approved AI product videos for the TikTok Shop creator (Mymobileworld ugc/*/out), hands-only, muted clips only.
- Day One AI YouTube channel @dayoneaitools1 (own channel, public videos and thumbnails).
- Diaspo Auto School AI receptionist (ElevenLabs agent) and lead page (jean-rameau repo), shown without name or number.
- Absent: no numbers (Ralph chose no numbers yet), no testimonials, no client logos, no prices. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Show the work before describing it.
2. Every claim is something already built and running.
3. One clear next step: the free consult form.
4. Fast and readable on a phone first.
